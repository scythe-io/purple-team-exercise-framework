## Ground Rules

Read these at the start of each session:

1. **Blameless culture** - Focus on processes, not people
2. **Speak freely** - All perspectives are valuable
3. **One conversation** - No side discussions
4. **Capture everything** - Ideas first, prioritize later
5. **Respect time** - Keep on schedule
6. **Action-oriented** - Every issue needs an owner
