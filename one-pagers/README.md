## Printing

These guides are designed to fit on a single page (US Letter or A4) when printed.

**Recommended print settings:**
- Paper size: Letter or A4
- Orientation: Portrait
- Margins: 0.5 inch / 1.25 cm
- Scale: Fit to page

## Customization

Feel free to adapt these templates to your organization's needs. Consider adding:
- Internal contact information
- Organization-specific procedures
- Custom metrics relevant to your environment
